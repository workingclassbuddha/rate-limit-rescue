# Rate Limit Rescue Privacy Policy

Rate Limit Rescue is a local-first Chrome extension for transferring working context between AI assistants.

## What The Extension Handles

When you ask the extension to capture a page, it may read:

- Recent messages from supported AI assistant pages
- Selected text or visible page text from the active tab
- Basic page metadata such as title and URL
- Rescue packs that you import, export, copy, insert, or delete

## Where Data Goes

Rate Limit Rescue does not run a server and does not sell or transfer user data to third parties. Context packs are created and stored locally in your browser using Chrome storage.

Your data leaves your browser only when you choose to copy, export, paste, insert, or send a handoff to another assistant or page.

## Permissions

The extension uses Chrome permissions to capture the active tab at your request, store recent rescue packs locally, open or focus the assistant you choose, and insert the generated handoff into supported assistant composers.
